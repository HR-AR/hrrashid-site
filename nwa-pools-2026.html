★ Top pick — closest & best

Rogers Aquatics Center

~8 min away, and the only real slide park near you: 5 slides off a 40-ft tower, a 500-ft lazy river, and a climbing wall. The smart buy is the 10-entry Splash Pass (✅ 2026: $99 for 48"+, $75 under 48") if you'll go 6+ times. Daily price and exact 2026 dates aren't posted on the city site yet — ⚠️ call 479-936-5482. It's already open for the season.

Worth the drive for serious sliders: Siloam Springs (two 130-ft slides + drop slides, ~43 min) and Prairie Grove (up to 4 slides — but ⚠️ confirm it reopened for 2026). Best unlimited-family value: Wilson Park season pass, $135. Cheapest close-in slide access: Springdale, $5/day.
